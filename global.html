Monster Spotted!!!!

Bermuda Island

In something you thought would be found in a fairy tale, Sailors have encountered the Mythical creature, said to have been a Myth for hundreds of years & is in the company of stories like Loch Ness Monster or the Flying Dutchman, but NO,The Kraken has been happened upon an unsuspecting cargo vessel!!! The Cargo Vessel said the Creature was Gigantic, easily capable of swallowing the entire vessel whole, it is said some of the sailors went into a coma just seeing the creature. Others are unable to speak and only babble in gibberish. One of the survivors say they managed to capture the Creature's likeness before it broke their Cargo Vessel in 2. (Not for sensitive viewers) The image is being released to the public, but be warned, it may cause nightmares for some.
