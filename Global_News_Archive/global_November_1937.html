Deal Made

Manila, Philippines 1st of November, 1937

In a diplomatic maneuver that has stunned the world and shifted the balance of power in the Pacific, the United States of America has formally completed the sale of the Philippines to the People's Republic of China. This unprecedented divestment of American territory comes as the U.S. pivots its entire military focus toward a deteriorating situation on the North American continent and an increasingly hostile Anglo-Japanese alliance.

The Pacific "Pressure Cooker"

The sale follows a month of rapidly deteriorating relations between Washington and the newly reaffirmed London-Tokyo axis. With the Imperial Japanese Navy and the British Royal Navy coordinating maneuvers, the American strategic position in the Philippines had become increasingly untenable— a distant outpost surrounded by the world's two most powerful fleets.

A New Era for China

For the Chinese government in Beijing, the acquisition of the Philippines represents a massive leap in regional status. While the purchase price remains a state secret, the transfer includes all existing American military infrastructure, giving China an immediate and formidable presence in the South China Sea.

As November closes, the Pacific is no longer an American lake. The sale of the Philippines marks the end of an era for the United States as a Far Eastern power, as it batches its hatches for a potential confrontation closer to home. The "Lady of the Seas" and the "Emperor of the Rising Sun" now look toward a North American coast that is increasingly isolated and defiant.

War!! The Sleeping Giant vs The Rising Sun & The Lady of the Seas

Washington D.C., United States of America 10th of November, 1937

In a stunning escalation of global tensions, the United States of America has officially declared war on the United Kingdom and Japan. This declaration comes in response to a long series of talks between London & Washington have stalled & failed. The United States after blockading the Western Canadian coast, in an effort to deny the USSR or any foreign power access to the Western Canadian coast, and completion of the deal between the USSR & British Empire, citing having Soviet troops on the Western Canadian coast as an unacceptable threat to American security. This however sparked outrage in London, citing that they are the owners of Canada & can do what they wish with it. The Blockade has since stranded a sizable number of British Troops in Western Canada, and has been a major point of contention between the two nations. The United States have said that the British troops stranded have not been blockaded and that they are free to leave, in an effort to calm hostilities, however the Queen has said those troops will not leave until the blockade has gone & USSR administrators are allowed in. This lead to the eventual decision by the United States to declare war on the United Kingdom to take Western Canada by force, to ensure no Soviet troops are stationed on the North American continent, Japan being in a Military Alliance with the United Kingdom has answered and accepted the call to aid the United Kingdom, and has declared war on the United States as well.

Potential World War? With the United States now at war with both the United Kingdom and Japan, the world stands on the brink of a global conflict with 3 World Powers going head-to-head. The Anglo-Japanese alliance has vowed to defend their interests in the Pacific and has called upon their allies to support their cause. Meanwhile, the United States is preparing for a protracted conflict that could reshape the global order.

War From South America to the Pacific

Amsterdam, Netherlands 6th of November, 1937

Out of Nowhere, Greece has declared war on the Netherlands, specifically targeting Suriname, a Dutch Colony in South America. This declaration shows Greece's ambitions to expand it's own influence in the western hempisphere, As recent conquests in Oman & Liberia shows that Greece itself is a rising power, trying to expand it's hold on resources and influence. However, were it a pure fight between the Netherlands and Greece, this would be a difficult fight to predict who would come out on top, however, Germany has declared war on the Netherlands in support of Greece, however more personal gain is suspected on the part of the Germans. As they have long wanted to right the wrongs of the past & unify all the Germanic regions under the German Reich, with Belgium already fallen & the Netherlands being the last major Germanic nation left in western Europe, this war is seen as a golden opportunity for Germany to achieve this goal, and has thrown their full support behind Greece in this conflict.

Hitler vows to unify the Netherlands under Germany:

In a statement to the German people Hitler said "The Netherlands is a nation that has long been a thorn in the side of the German people, with its refusal to unify with the German Reich and its history of siding with our enemies. This war is an opportunity to right the wrongs of the past and bring the Dutch people into the fold of the German Reich, where they belong. We will not rest until the Netherlands is unified with Germany and the German people are stronger than ever before." This makes more sense as the German Second Fleet was spotted last month leaving Bremen & heading to it's holdings in the Pacific. The German force apparently on the border of the Netherlands has doubled from the one in Belgium, showing that, that conflict has not weakend them. Their troops in the Pacific also stand poised to take the Dutch East Indies, to complete this invasion.

With Both Greece & Germany declaring war on the Netherlands, the Dutch are in a precarious position, with their forces stretched thin across multiple fronts. It seems highly unlikely that the Dutch will be able to hold off both the Greek and German forces, and the fall of the Netherlands seems imminent. The world watches with bated breath as this conflict unfolds.
